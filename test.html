**Title:** A $100/Month Experiment in AI-Assisted Self-Reinvention (Publicly Documented)

**Summary**
I am proposing a small, time-bounded experiment: can a person meaningfully upgrade their skills and trajectory using only $100/month, AI tools, and disciplined daily execution?

I am requesting $100/month for 3–6 months to run this experiment and document it publicly.

---

**Why This Is Interesting**
Many people are stuck in low-leverage jobs not because of lack of intelligence or motivation, but because they lack:

* a structured transition period
* a clear method
* proof that change is possible with limited resources

This project aims to produce a concrete, real-world example of low-cost self-reinvention.

---

**What I Will Do**

I will treat this as a full-time project with daily output. The system has three parts:

### 1. AI-Assisted English Skill Building

* Daily speaking and writing using AI feedback loops
* Focus on practical communication ability
* Output: daily English posts

### 2. Applied Marketing Experiments

* Learn and apply copywriting and distribution
* Run small-scale experiments (content, growth, messaging)
* Use AI to accelerate iteration

### 3. Build in Public

* Document everything: process, mistakes, results
* Publish daily/weekly updates
* No “silent learning” — only visible progress

---

**Constraints (Intentional Design)**

This is intentionally constrained:

* Budget: $100/month
* Time: 3–6 months
* Tools: primarily AI + internet

The constraint is the point — to test what is possible under minimal conditions.

---

**Outputs**

By the end of the experiment, I will produce:

* 100+ public posts documenting the process
* A working English communication baseline
* A set of real marketing experiments with results
* A final write-up:
  *“How to Rebuild Skills with $100/Month (Using AI)”*

---

**Why Me**

I am currently at a point of transition. My current work is not sustainable, and I need to redirect my path.

Rather than drifting, I am choosing to run a structured experiment with strict discipline and public accountability.

I am not asking for comfort — I am asking for a constraint that forces execution.

---

**Use of Funds**

$100/month will be used for:

* minimal living gap during transition
* basic tools / subscriptions
* maintaining uninterrupted focus

---

**Execution Commitment**

* Daily output (no zero days)

* Transparent tracking of progress

---

**Long-Term Value**

If successful, this becomes:

* A replicable model for low-cost self-improvement
* A public resource for people in similar situations
* A foundation for future higher-impact work

---

**Closing**

This is a small bet with asymmetric upside.
If it works, it produces a concrete example of how minimal resources + modern tools can unlock meaningful change.
